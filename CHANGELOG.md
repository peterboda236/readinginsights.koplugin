# Changelog

## v6.1.0

### Reading streak popup
- Tapping any streak on the insights page - or "Show Reading streak" in the menu - now opens one combined popup: current and best streak side by side (name, date range, days | weeks) above the calendar.
- The calendar shows your whole reading history back to your first reading, with every read day marked and each week's streak shaded.

## v6.0.0

### Reading streak popups
- Tapping a streak now opens a calendar of that streak's span.
- The calendar is a fixed six-week month. It pages one month at a time (‹ / › arrows, swipe, or Left/Right keys).
- Read days are marked in the daily-streak color.
- Once a week has any reading, its remaining days are shaded (the weekly streak is secured for that week).
- Days within a week sit flush, so a run of reading reads as one solid strip.
- Days from the previous/next month are shown in gray.
- Today's date is bold.
- The popup shows the days/weeks-in-a-row pair and the book count in three equal columns.
- The streak name is on the left, the date range right-aligned, with no divider between them.
- The total reading time and per-day averages are hidden by default. A new setting turns them back on (Settings ▸ Advanced settings ▸ Reading insight popup ▸ Streak popup reading time).

### Popup widths
- The streak, book progress calendar, heatmap and 8-week trend popups all open at a fixed 94% of screen width.
- The Records popup still sizes to its content, now capped at 94% of screen width.

### Colors
- New settings under **Colors ▸ Reading streak calendar**: daily-streak day and weekly-streak gap day.
- Non-streak days are always white. Day numbers pick black or white automatically for contrast.

### Week start
- The weekly streak value now follows the **week start day** setting (Monday or Sunday) instead of always Monday.
- The streak calendar's weeks follow the same setting.
- The "most read in a week" achievement follows the same setting.

### Book progress calendar
- The month grid is now a fixed six weeks.
- Days from the previous/next month are shown in gray.
- Day cells no longer have borders; they sit flush, and today is marked by its bold day number.
